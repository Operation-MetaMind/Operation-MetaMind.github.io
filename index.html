About Our Organization

Operation MetaMind is an independent research organization dedicated to producing high-quality evidence syntheses in clinical and health sciences. Our core expertise lies in conducting Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses (SRMA) that follow PRISMA guidelines and Cochrane methodology, contributing to the global body of evidence-based medical knowledge.

We bring together physicians, medical researchers, and trainees across multiple institutions, united by a commitment to methodological rigor, transparent research practices, and open science. Our structured task force model enables efficient collaboration across all phases of the systematic review process.

As an independent organization, we maintain formal data use agreements, ethical oversight standards, and security protocols required for accessing restricted research databases from agencies including the National Cancer Institute (NCI), SEER Program, dbGaP, and other national repositories.

Core Research Focus

Our research program integrates clinical inquiry with modern evidence synthesis methodologies to address critical questions in health sciences.

Systematic Reviews & Meta-Analyses

Our primary methodology: comprehensive evidence synthesis following PRISMA 2020 guidelines and Cochrane protocols. We conduct rigorous searches, transparent screening, and quantitative pooling of clinical evidence.

Clinical Outcomes Research

Comparative effectiveness studies evaluating surgical techniques, treatment modalities, and clinical interventions. We focus on patient-centered outcomes including survival, morbidity, and quality of life.

Esophageal Cancer Research

Our inaugural project compares Robot-Assisted Minimally Invasive Esophagectomy (RAMIE) versus conventional Minimally Invasive Esophagectomy (MIE) through a comprehensive meta-analysis of clinical outcomes.

Computational Research Methods

Leveraging modern statistical tools (R, RevMan, JASP) for quantitative synthesis, risk of bias assessment (RoB 2), and advanced analytical frameworks for heterogeneity analysis and sensitivity testing.

Data Governance & Research Ethics

Operation MetaMind is committed to the highest standards of responsible research conduct and data stewardship. We adhere to all applicable data use agreements, institutional review requirements, and ethical guidelines governing the use of sensitive research data.

Data Use Agreements

All research involving restricted or controlled-access datasets is conducted under formally executed data use agreements. Our researchers are trained in the terms, conditions, and security requirements specific to each dataset.

Ethical Oversight

Research protocols involving human subjects data are reviewed through appropriate ethical oversight mechanisms. We maintain documentation of all approvals, exemptions, and compliance certifications.

Data Security

We implement rigorous technical and administrative safeguards to protect the confidentiality and integrity of research data, consistent with NIST and institutional security frameworks.

Reproducibility & Open Science

We are committed to transparent and reproducible research. Code, methodologies, and non-restricted results are shared through public repositories and peer-reviewed publications.
