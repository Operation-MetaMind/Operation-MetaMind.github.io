01The model

We train researchers, not just produce research.

Every member is taught to lead a study end to end, from protocol to publication. Method, mentorship, and authorship are structured so that capability compounds with each project.

  • Method-led

    We develop and teach our own workflows for screening, appraisal, and synthesis.

  • Authorship by contribution

    Order follows ICMJE and CRediT, determined by the work.

  • Open by default

    Protocols are registered before analysis. Code and results are shared where permitted.

The questions we are closing.

RAMIEAccepted

Is robotic esophagectomy actually better than the standard approach?

ACS Clinical Congress 2026. Fast-tracked for JACS.

VEMURAFAccepted

Does a BRAF inhibitor work in a leukemia too rare to run a trial on?

A proportional meta-analysis of response and survival with vemurafenib in resistant hairy cell leukemia.

SOHO 2026. Registered on PROSPERO, in screening.

CHIMNEYIn development

Does prophylactic mesh prevent parastomal hernia, or only delay it?

A meta-analysis with trial sequential analysis, pooling long-term follow-up the guidelines were written without.

Research Hackathon 2026 finalist. Targeting ACS 2027.

Anl_CARegistered

Has anal cancer treatment actually changed across three decades?

A SEER population-based cohort of treatment trends and survival, 2000 to 2023.

Registered on OSF. Journal article in progress.

SEERSubmitted

Does Commission on Cancer accreditation improve resection and survival in gastric cancer?

A SEER population-based cohort of resectable gastric adenocarcinoma, accredited versus non-accredited centers.

SEERSubmitted

Abstract submitted, ACS Clinical Congress 2026.

SEERSubmitted
